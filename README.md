I'm a **B.Tech Computer Science student** who enjoys building projects that combine **technology, creativity, and storytelling**.

Instead of creating another traditional informational website, I wanted to challenge myself by building something that felt immersive—something users would **experience**, not just browse.

That idea eventually became **Bubbleverse**.

---

# 🌟 About Bubbleverse

Bubbleverse is an interactive, cinematic web experience that takes users on a scroll-driven journey through the world of Bubble Tea.

Every flavour is presented as its own chapter with:

- 🎬 Cinematic animations
- 🎨 Unique visual themes
- 🌍 Educational storytelling
- 🍵 Ingredient highlights
- ✨ Smooth transitions
- 💫 Interactive motion

Rather than clicking through pages, users naturally **discover** each flavour as they continue scrolling.

---

# 💡 The Inspiration

The project started as a simple Bubble Tea website.

While developing it, I became fascinated by premium storytelling websites like Apple's product pages and modern interactive editorial experiences.

That completely changed my direction.

Instead of making users navigate a website...

I wanted them to **feel like they were walking through a digital exhibition dedicated to Bubble Tea.**

---

# 🚀 What I Learned

This project pushed me far beyond traditional frontend development.

## 🎬 Scroll-based Storytelling

I learned how scrolling itself can become part of the narrative instead of simply moving down a webpage.

---

## 🖼 Frame-by-frame Animation

One of the biggest challenges was experimenting with frame sequence rendering.

Instead of embedding videos directly, I converted animations into hundreds of image frames to create smooth cinematic experiences.

---

## 🎨 Motion Design

Throughout this project I explored:

- Smooth transitions
- Scroll choreography
- Micro interactions
- Dynamic colour themes
- Layered animations
- Interactive storytelling

---

## 🎯 UI / UX Design

I gained a much better understanding of:

- Typography
- Layout composition
- Colour harmony
- Visual hierarchy
- User flow
- Storytelling through design

Small details like spacing and animation timing ended up making a huge difference.
